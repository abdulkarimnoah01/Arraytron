# Falloff from the shape of a mask, mask export, and the Scatter tab (v1.32.0)

## Falloff follows any mask shape
Falloff used to fade from a centre outwards, as a circle or a box around the layout (or around each cluster, or along each paint stroke). **Shape: Mask Shape** (Scatter tab, FALLOFF) fades from the **edge of the Mask** instead, so it follows any shape and any boundary: a painted crescent, an L, a noise blob, an image, the mask painted with Houdini's Attribute Paint.

* **Fade Depth** (default **Whole shape**): the fade runs from the deepest point of the shape (the middle of every blob, strip or piece of it) to its edge, so the scale and the density grade smoothly ACROSS the whole shape, like a Radial falloff that follows the shape, and every piece of a shape fades over its own size. **Fixed width** fades only the Fade Width next to the edge and keeps the rest at full size and density.
* **Keep Points Beyond The Fade** (default off): the points beyond the fade (outside the shape, or farther than the Fade Width outside it) are REMOVED, so Edge Density can stay at 100 and only the SCALE falls towards the edge; on, they stay at the edge density and the edge scale (a dust of tiny instances all over the layout when the density is high).
* **Fade** (default Inside the shape): *Inside* is full size and density deep inside the shape and fades to nothing at its edge (none outside); *Outside* is full inside and feathers out beyond the edge; *Both sides of the edge* is half at the edge, full a Fade Width inside and nothing a Fade Width outside.
* **Fade Width** (default 2): the distance, in scene units, the fade takes from the edge (not used with Fade Depth Whole shape and Fade Inside; with Both sides it is the outside part). For a scale that fades gently and keeps the points, set Edge Density to 100 and Edge Scale to the size you want at the edge. **Mask Level** (default 0.5): the shape is where the Mask weight (after Invert and the Low / High / Gamma remap of the Mask section) is at least this. The Curve, Flat Centre, Edge Density and Edge Scale of the Falloff work as before.
* **Works with every Mask Source**, also the real-time painted mask: paint, and the fade follows the brush. The Mask is looked at from above on a grid over the X and Z extent of the points (at most 160 cells along the longer side, a fade at most 16 cells wide) at the mean height of the points; the distance to the edge is exact (a separable Euclidean distance transform over the grid). A 3D noise mask is therefore read at one height (the painted, image, mesh primvar and Houdini-paint masks are flat to begin with).
* Without a Mask Source the node says so ("choose a Mask Source"). The Region, Axes, Centre and Reach settings do not apply to the Mask shape and are hidden. Radial and Box are exactly the previous release.

### Recipe: a smooth scale gradient that follows the shape
Mask Source Painted (or Houdini Paint, noise, an image), **Mask Effect Scale with Min Scale 1** (so the paint only gives the SHAPE and does not remove or shrink points by itself), a dense scatter (raise the Count or Density and, in Surface mode, the Point Limit), then Falloff **Shape Mask Shape, Fade Depth Whole shape, Fade Inside, Curve Smooth, Flat Centre 0, Edge Density 100, Edge Scale 0.05**: the biggest instances are in the middle of the shape, they shrink smoothly to a fine dust at its edge, every point inside is kept and none is left beyond it. The simple setup of this recipe (rubber toys on a grid, a Houdini-painted crescent) is `otls_h205/Arraytron_v1.32.0_H205_maskshape_demo.hip`; `maskshape_crescent_demo.hip` is the built one (Inside, Both sides, baked).

## Edge Fade and the instances following the mask
The Mask section (any Mask Source, the painted mask and the **Houdini Paint** included) has **Edge Fade**: re-fade the EDGE of the mask with a fade you control, whatever softness it was painted with.

* **Edge Fade** (off by default), **Fade Width** (scene units, default 2: the transition from full weight to none, centred on the edge), **Grow / Shrink** (moves the edge out or in), **Fade Curve** (Linear, Smooth, Gaussian) and, in Mask Details, **Fade Level** (which mask value is the edge, default 0.5) and **Max Grid Cells** (default 256). A hard paint and a very soft one give the same mask after Edge Fade, a narrow width is a crisp edge and a wide one a long even slope. The edge is measured on a fixed lattice (about six cells across the width), so a point gets the same weight whatever the other points are.
* **The density and the scale follow the mask**: Mask Effect **Density + Scale**, Min Scale 0: a point survives with a probability equal to the weight and its scale IS the weight (checked to a thousandth). **Min Scale** lifts the floor of the scale. **Scale Response Curve** (new) gives the SCALE a curve of its own of the weight (flip it, make a hinge or a plateau) while the density keeps following the weight itself.
* After Edge Fade the usual Invert, Low / High, Gamma, Remap Curve and Output Range shape the new weight; Visualize and Falloff Mask Shape see the re-faded mask.
* Demo: `tools/build_demo_maskfade_v1320.py` (`dist/Arraytron_v1.32.0_maskfade_demo.hip`): a soft S-shaped Houdini Paint stroke as painted (density and scale follow the mask), with a crisp Edge Fade and with a long one plus a Scale Response curve.

## Save, Cache And Export the mask
In the MASK section, a collapsible **Save, Cache And Export** (any Mask Source except None):

* **Export Mask Image**: writes the RAW mask weight (before Invert and the remap), seen from above, as a **16 bit grey PNG** (no library needed) or an **.exr / .tif** (OpenImageIO). **Mask File** (default `$HIP/arraytron_mask.png`), **Image Size** (the longer side, default 1024), **Export Area**: Automatic (the painted extent, the baked Houdini paint map, or the generated points, plus a tenth) or Custom (the Center and Size of the Image source). Row 0 of the image is -Z, column 0 is -X, like the Image mask's Top projection.
* **Bake To Image**: writes that image and makes it the **Mask Source (Image, Top projection over the same area, edge pixels clamped)**: the paint or the noise is no longer evaluated, the mask is a frozen, fast, shareable cache, and Invert, Low / High, Gamma and the curve keep working on it. Undoable. (A baked 1024 pixel map reproduces the painted points to within a pixel: 99.8 percent of the same points in the demo.)
* **Save Paint / Load Paint** (Painted and Houdini Paint sources): the brush stamps or the baked Houdini paint map, with Unpainted Value, Map Resolution and Map Feather, as a `.json` that loads into this or another node or scene. Load Paint makes the node's source Painted or Houdini Paint. Undoable.

## The Scatter tab
The **Layout** tab is now the **Scatter** tab and holds, in the order they work: the Distribution Mode and its settings, Move The Layout, the **MASK**, the **FIELD STACK** and the **FALLOFF**. The **Variation** tab keeps the Individual Instances, the Randomize and the Noise Positional Shift. Parameter names do not change, so scenes, scripts and presets work as before; only the places of the Mask and the Field Stack moved. The previous release keeps its tabs.
